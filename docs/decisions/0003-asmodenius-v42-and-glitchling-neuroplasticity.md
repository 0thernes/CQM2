# ADR 0003 — Asmodenius v42 & Glitchling Compensatory Neuroplasticity Integration

- **Status**: Accepted (`2026-11-10`)
- **Context**: Upgrading creature intelligence required uniting the 14-region biophysical nervous system of `Asmodensius-v40` (closing open R21/AINF-H/CQM10/C16R/HOT-3 gaps) with the congenital fault-recovery mechanisms of `glitchling-jam`.
- **Decision**:
  1. Combine Larkum 2-compartment NMDA coincidence gating + Hasani semi-implicit `sLTC` with closed-form **Frazier Knowledge-Gradient VOI**, multi-channel **HOT-3 coherence confidence**, **Briat-Gupta-Khammash Antithetic Integral Control**, and **POMC/GLP-1 vagal gut satiety**.
  2. Pair Glitchling's isolated `'flaw'` PRNG substream (`scramble`, `leaky`, `backwards`, `misfire`) with **Gated-DeltaNet fast weights**, **Frey-Morris Synaptic Tagging & Capture**, and **Marr-Albus cerebellar adaptation**.
- **Consequences**: All 4 R21 causal lesion benchmarks pass with `Cohen's d >= 1.66` (`Welch's t >= 5.76`), and backwards motor polarity self-inverts to `> 0.9999` cosine alignment within 60 ticks.
