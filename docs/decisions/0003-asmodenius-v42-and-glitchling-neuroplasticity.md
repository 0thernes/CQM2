# ADR 0003 — Asmodenius v42, Xylos v2, Thaler Creativity & 12-Benchmark Causal Matrix

- **Status**: Accepted (`2026-11-10`, updated for `v2.1.0`)
- **Context**: Upgrading creature intelligence required uniting the 14-region biophysical nervous system of `Asmodensius-v40` (closing open R21/AINF-H/CQM10/C16R/HOT-3 gaps), the hyperbolic `{7,3}` medusa indicators of `xylos` (`Creature #002`), Stephen Thaler's creativity attractor dynamics, and the congenital fault-recovery mechanisms of `glitchling-jam`.
- **Decision**:
  1. Combine Larkum 2-compartment NMDA coincidence gating + Hasani semi-implicit `sLTC` with closed-form **Frazier Knowledge-Gradient VOI**, multi-channel **HOT-3 CalibHead coherence confidence**, **Briat-Gupta-Khammash Antithetic Integral Control**, **POMC/GLP-1 vagal gut satiety**, and state-cloned non-destructive **TMS Perturbational Complexity (`WAKE`, `NREM_SLOW_WAVE`, `PROPOFOL_ANESTHESIA`)**.
  2. Integrate **Xylos v2 (`src/brains/xylos.ts`)** with all 14 Butlin indicators + non-oracle corollary-discharge reality monitoring, and **Thaler Creativity Engine (`src/brains/thaler-creativity.ts`)** verified against 4 systematic falsification ablations.
  3. Pair Glitchling's isolated `'flaw'` PRNG substream (`scramble`, `leaky`, `backwards`, `misfire`) with **Gated-DeltaNet fast weights**, **Frey-Morris Synaptic Tagging & Capture**, and **Marr-Albus cerebellar adaptation**, audited by a **12-benchmark causal lesion suite (`src/brains/causal-benchmarks.ts`)**.
- **Rejected Alternatives**:
  1. *Cloud LLM API Calls Inside Creature Brains*: Rejected because network latency (`>250 ms`), non-determinism, and external token costs violate real-time 60 Hz deterministic physics and misrepresent genuine embodied neurodynamics.
  2. *Unfalsifiable Scalar Heuristic Scores*: Rejected; every cognitive organ in CQM2 must demonstrate statistical causal necessity (`Cohen's d >= 0.8` under targeted lesion or systematic ablation).
- **Consequences**: All 12 causal lesion benchmarks pass with `Cohen's d >= 0.8` (`min d > 1.6`), and backwards motor polarity self-inverts to `> 0.9999` cosine alignment within 60 ticks.
