CQM2 Scientific & Systems Glossary

Antithetic Integral Controller (AIC)
Biomolecular feedback motif (Briat, Gupta & Khammash, 2016) using mutual annihilation (η·z₁·z₂) to guarantee robust perfect adaptation of blood glucose to μ/θ = 4.0 kJ.
Asmodenius v42 (Creature #1)
Hero artificial-life organism uniting a 14-region recurrent biophysical nervous system + Enteric Nervous System, Larkum 2-compartment NMDA gating, Frazier VOI foraging, HOT-3 confidence, and N13 somatic Free-Won't veto.
Entity Relational Programming (ERP)
Normalized data-oriented architecture organizing all simulation state into 10 foreign-key-constrained Structure-of-Arrays (SoA) tables mirrored in 3NF SQLite WAL.
Frazier Knowledge-Gradient VOI
Closed-form Bayesian Value-of-Information metric quantifying expected decision-utility gain from sampling a noisy environmental cue prior to irreversible action.
Frey-Morris Synaptic Tagging & Capture (STC)
Two-stage memory consolidation mechanism where transient early-LTP sets synaptic eligibility tags that capture Dopamine/NE-triggered Plasticity-Related Proteins into decay-resistant late-LTP.
Glitchling Congenital Flaws
Four heritable/toggleable sensorimotor and synaptic deficits (scramble, leaky, backwards, misfire) overcome online by Gated-DeltaNet, Frey-Morris STC, and Marr-Albus cerebellar adaptation.
Glossodrom RS[15, 11] Zooid Codec
Systematic Reed-Solomon error-correcting xenolinguistic code over GF(16) mapped stereographically from the 4D Clifford torus on S³ into 3D bioluminescent filaments.
Hoggar Split Cayley Hexagon H(2)
63-line contextual subgeometry inside 3-qubit symplectic polar space W(5, 2) detected via Fubini-Study metric violation |g_μν| = 1/9 > 1/12 on the Hoggar fiducial state.
